New Student Guide

Coming from PNG to South Dakota?

This guide can help new students prepare for travel, arrival, and their first semester at SDSU.

01

Before You Leave PNG

Review your passport, visa documents, I-20, university correspondence, and travel plans.

02

Traveling to South Dakota

Plan your international route and final connection to Sioux Falls or Brookings.

03

Prepare for Winter

South Dakota winters are significantly colder than PNG. Prepare appropriate clothing after arrival.

04

Campus Life

Learn about housing, dining, transportation, campus employment, and student organizations.

05

International Student Support

Stay connected with SDSU international student services for immigration and enrollment requirements.

06

Connect With PNGSA

PNGSA can help new students meet other PNG students and settle into life in Brookings.

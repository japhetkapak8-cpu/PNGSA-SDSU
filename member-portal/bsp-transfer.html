PNG STUDENT MONEY GUIDE

BSP PNG → U.S. Bank Transfer

A practical step-by-step guide for Papua New Guinean students transferring money from BSP Papua New Guinea to First Bank & Trust in Brookings, South Dakota.

Usually 1–3 business days BSP online FX fee: K40* U.S. Dollar
Verify your U.S. wire instructions before sending money.

Do not guess a SWIFT/BIC, routing number, Agent ID, intermediary bank, or beneficiary account number. Ask First Bank & Trust for its current incoming international USD wire instructions for your account.

Sending Bank BSP Papua New Guinea
Receiving Bank First Bank & Trust
Destination Brookings, South Dakota
Currency USD

1
BSP ONLINE BANKING

Open BSP Online Banking

Go to the official BSP website and choose Login → BSP Online Banking. Sign in using your own BSP Online Banking credentials.

Use the official BSP website or a trusted bookmark. Never enter your banking password through a link sent by an unknown person.

2
PAYMENTS

Open Transfer Money

After signing in, open the Payments menu and select Transfer Money. Start a new transfer and choose the international payment option.

Payments Transfer Money International
3
INTERNATIONAL PAYMENT

Select the Destination and Currency

On the Adhoc International Payment screen, select the destination and currency that match the U.S. transfer.

Account Type International
To Country United States
To Currency US Dollar (USD)
Industry Code 0000 – Private Individuals & Households*

*Choose the option that truthfully matches the transaction. BSP/BPNG reporting selections can depend on the purpose and circumstances of the transfer.

4
PURPOSE & FINANCIAL DETAILS

Enter the Transfer Purpose and Amount

Select the purpose that accurately describes why the money is being transferred. BSP's form may include options such as education, travel, transfers, or transfer to an offshore account.

Purpose Select the truthful purpose
Transfer Description Briefly describe the payment
Transfer From Your BSP account
Amount Enter the amount to transfer

For student-related funds, choose the category that actually describes the transaction rather than selecting a category only because it appears convenient.

5
BENEFICIARY BANK

Enter First Bank & Trust Wire Information

Use the incoming international wire instructions provided directly by First Bank & Trust. The information needed may include the receiving bank, routing/Agent ID, SWIFT/BIC if applicable, and an intermediary or correspondent bank.

Do not copy a routing or SWIFT code from an unofficial website.

International wire routing can differ from ordinary domestic ACH information. Confirm the exact instructions for your account.

6
BENEFICIARY

Enter Your U.S. Account Details

Complete the beneficiary information exactly as it appears on the receiving account.

7
REVIEW

Review Everything Before Submitting

International transfers can be difficult or expensive to correct. Carefully review every field before authorizing the payment.

COST & TIMING

How Long and How Much?

Typical BSP Transfer Time 1–3 business days

Correspondent banks, weekends, holidays, compliance reviews, or incorrect details can cause delays.

BSP Online FX Payment Fee K40*

Check BSP's current fee schedule before sending because fees can change.

Total Economic Cost May be more than K40

Exchange-rate conversion and intermediary or receiving-bank charges may increase the total cost.
